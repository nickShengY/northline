Workflow guide

Fishing crew safety and operational readiness

Northline brings hazard reporting, incident workflows, training records, and trip context into the same operations platform. Field crews record activity in mobile workflows while coordinators review risk and open incidents in the web portal.

Offshore crew workflows

The mobile offshore mode includes haul safety checks and gear transitions. On the vessel, the tablet app provides a watch handoff checklist, bridge log, vessel status, and gear board. These surfaces help a crew pass operational context between shifts and between the bridge and deck.

Ice fishing operations

Northline also has an ice mode with check-in flows, return plans, and heater reminders. Organizers can use these records alongside hazards and training activity to keep the group's plan and reported status in view.

A handoff worth reviewing

  1. Review the current trip or group status.
  2. Check open hazards and incidents with their latest context.
  3. Review the gear state and outstanding field actions.
  4. Confirm relevant training completion and readiness records.
  5. Check sync state so the next shift knows how current the information is.

Training alongside operations

Northline includes training assignment, completion, and recommendation workflows. Keeping those records near incident and trip review helps coordinators connect follow-up work to the people and activities involved.

How does risk review fit into daily work?

The portal brings risk scoring and incident monitoring together with trip state. Teams can inspect the underlying hazards and activity when reviewing a score, then record follow-up through their incident workflow. Keeping the source context visible makes the record useful during a handoff and during a later review.
